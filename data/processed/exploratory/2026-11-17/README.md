## Source gaps / not established

- **Launch-week OnChain yields.** The five OnChain records dated 2025-08-04, 2025-08-05, 2025-08-06, 2025-08-07, 2025-08-08 are filed as 0.0001 (0.01%) while Liquidity reports 4.15% and the series gross yield 4.33% on the same dates. No held source states Fidelity's seven-day yield convention for a new class's first week (for example, whether a yield is computed over fewer than seven days of operation or set near zero). The audited Financial Highlights give the OnChain period as beginning August 4, 2025 (commencement of sale of shares). The records are reported here as filed; they are not treated as data errors and no mechanism is assigned. Held filings checked by the independent audit (`workflow/reviews/SUPERVISOR17_ECON_AUDIT.md`, AU-03) bear on their interpretation: OnChain's only August 2025 flows were $500,000 subscriptions on 4 and 12 August; month-end shares of 1,002,691.87 imply $2,691.87 of growth outside reported flows, consistent with accrual at about 4.09% from launch rather than near zero; the 2 September 2025 cross-class match differs by exactly that amount; the first non-0.01% value (11 August) is the first business day seven or more days after launch, and Liquidity reported no yields for its first seven days (23-27 December 2024); and OnChain was 100% held of record by an affiliated entity in August 2025. The launch records are therefore consistent with an incomplete seven-day calculation window; the convention itself is not documented in held sources. They are not evidence of an income shortfall.
- **August 2025 flat yields.** On every paired date 2025-08-04..2025-08-29 the Liquidity net yield takes the value(s) 4.15% and the series gross yield 4.33% (2025-08-01: 4.14% and 4.32%); OnChain takes 4.09% on every date 2025-08-11..2025-08-29. The OnChain gross-minus-net over 2025-08-11..29 is 0.24 pp against 0.18 pp for Liquidity, above OnChain's later level (about 0.20 pp) and its current 0.20% cap. No held source explains this; the fee history before the June 29, 2026 prospectus is not held (earlier prospectuses/supplements were not acquired).
- **Fee history.** The fee tables and caps are those of the prospectus dated June 29, 2026 (cap through August 31, 2027). The audited Financial Highlights give realised ratios only for the fiscal periods ended April 30, 2025 and 2026. Effective caps on earlier dates are not established.
- **Conversions.** N-MFP3 does not label class conversions. Same-day opposite cross-class flows above $1m matching within 1% are listed (4 dates) as consistent with conversion only. The Liquidity prospectus (Converting Shares) describes Liquidity-to-OnChain conversion; no held prospectus text describes OnChain-to-Liquidity conversion (the OnChain prospectus has no conversion text), so the OnChain-redemption/Liquidity-subscription match(es) on 2026-02-04 are not explained by the held documents. The OnChain SAI's generic statement that "The shares have no preemptive or conversion rights" does not explain it either.
- **Balances.** End-of-day balances are reconstructed from month-end shares and daily gross flows; dividend reinvestment timing and cash distributions are not separately reported, so balances, balance weights, income illustrations and annualised waiver rates are approximate.
- **Item B.9.a scope.** The filed waiver dollars cover any person's payment or waiver of fund operating expenses for the class in the month; the filings do not split them by type. Blockchain transaction fees are stated to be borne by the Adviser or its affiliates; no held source quantifies them.
- **Cross-fund gross-minus-net.** Rows labelled cross-fund context compare different funds with different portfolios, fees and waivers; they are not a tokenisation premium.
