# Manuscript evidence map

## Scope and identifiers

The current manuscript is `drafts/independent/dissertation_revised.tex`, with `references_revised.bib` beside it. The numerical index is `data/processed/key_numbers.csv`: its `name`, `value`, `unit`, `as_of`, `source_file` and `script` columns lead to the underlying outputs. Some exploratory results are cited directly from their CSVs and are not all appended to that index.

Fund identifiers: FOBXX/BENJI (Franklin), WTGXX/WTGX on Stellar (WisdomTree), JLTXX (JPMorgan), and FYOXX/FDIT (Fidelity OnChain class). Fidelity's FDIT contract address is not confirmed by an issuer source; its chain interpretation remains conditional. Fidelity Liquidity class C000255665 is the descriptive comparison with OnChain C000261149.

The main window is 1 January to 30 September 2026 in America/New_York time. The July to September calibration window (called the feasibility pilot in earlier records) informed the thresholds and implementation. Earlier-month tests therefore assess a partly calibrated specification; they are not a wholly prospective test. Missing Aptos history prevents complete Franklin reconciliation. A missing chain is not treated as zero.
